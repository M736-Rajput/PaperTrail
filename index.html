About the PaperTrail Prototype

What is PaperTrail?

PaperTrail is a prototype designed to bridge the gap between scientific hypotheses and published, peer-reviewed literature. Researchers, clinicians, and curious minds can enter natural language questions to query scientific databases and evaluate evidence.

Current Capability: Live Paper Discovery

PaperTrail is currently configured as a live paper discovery engine powered by the Europe PMC REST API (the European Bioinformatics Institute database covering over 40 million articles, preprints, and patents).

  • Searches biomedical & life-sciences publications in real-time.
  • Retrieves paper titles, author rosters, journal metrics, abstracts, and DOI/PMCID links.
  • Filters by Open Access status, publication year, and review articles.

Roadmap: Automated Evidence Synthesis

In upcoming phases, PaperTrail will integrate AI reasoning models to:

  1. Extract atomic, testable claims from user questions.
  2. Analyze study methodologies (Randomized Controlled Trials vs. Observational vs. In Vitro).
  3. Classify each study's findings as Supporting, Contradicting, or Weakening the claim.
  4. Generate transparent consensus summaries with confidence intervals.
